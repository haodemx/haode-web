# Facebook iPhone 11 Traffic Ad Previews Design

## Goal

Create three square preview ads for a Facebook traffic campaign that sends repair shops, technicians, wholesalers, and retailers in Mexico directly to the HAODE App product page for `iphone-incell-11-bolsa-protectora`.

## Confirmed product facts

- Product: `Pantalla para iPhone 11 Bolsa Protectora`
- Quality: `INCELL FHD`
- Confirmed source image: `assets/products/iphone-incell/11-bolsa-protectora/main.jpg`
- Confirmed HAODE logo: `assets/logo/logo.png`
- Public single-piece price remains `$160 MXN`.
- The advertised `$140 MXN por pieza` is only the confirmed `Caja/modelo` price.
- Landing URL: `https://haode.com.mx/app/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=iphone11_bolsa_140&utm_content=facebook_ad_square#producto/iphone-incell-11-bolsa-protectora`
- The website supports cart and ERP order submission, followed by WhatsApp confirmation. It does not support online payment.

## Required copy

All customer-visible copy is Mexican Spanish. Each preview must contain these exact ideas without changing the price condition:

- `PANTALLA PARA iPHONE 11`
- `INCELL FHD · BOLSA PROTECTORA`
- `DESDE $140 MXN / PIEZA`
- `PRECIO CAJA/MODELO`
- `COMPRA EN NUESTRO SITIO`

Do not claim confirmed stock, warranty terms, urgency, discount percentage, free shipping, or online payment.

## Preview concepts

### Concept A: HAODE conversion orange

A clean orange, white, and dark-navy B2B layout. The HAODE logo sits in the upper-left safe area, the confirmed product dominates the center-right, the price is the strongest visual element, and the website CTA appears at the bottom.

### Concept B: Product-first premium

A dark-navy premium layout with restrained orange lighting. The confirmed iPhone 11 product remains the hero, the price is prominent but secondary to model recognition, and the page feels credible to repair shops and technicians.

### Concept C: Price-first retail energy

A high-energy orange and blue composition with a large `$140` price block and a clear `PRECIO CAJA/MODELO` qualifier directly beside it. The CTA is obvious without implying online payment.

## Production constraints

- Format: square Facebook feed preview, `1080 × 1080` target composition.
- Use the confirmed product image as the product reference and the confirmed logo as the brand reference.
- Preserve the iPhone 11 bag, screen, packaging, and product identity. Do not replace them with a generic phone or a different model.
- Keep text and logo inside safe margins.
- Do not publish, schedule, or spend advertising budget during preview production.
- Do not modify website code, product data, prices, images, or the live Facebook account.

## Review and verification

Each preview is inspected for product identity, exact price condition, Spanish spelling, HAODE logo fidelity, CTA clarity, and absence of unsupported claims. Any preview with altered product identity, unreadable text, or missing `PRECIO CAJA/MODELO` is rejected or regenerated.
