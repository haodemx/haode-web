# HAODE CRM Sales

Use this skill for lead lists, WhatsApp scripts, and sales tracking.

## Rules

- Write sales copy in Spanish by default.
- Segment leads as `Menudeo`, `Mayoreo`, `Distribuidor`, or `Socio por ciudad`.
- Default follow-up statuses:
  - `Nuevo`
  - `Cotizado`
  - `En seguimiento`
  - `Cliente`
  - `Pendiente de pago`
  - `Enviado`
  - `Posventa`
- Create spreadsheet-ready templates when useful.
- Keep outreach practical, respectful, and focused on repair shops and wholesalers in Mexico.
