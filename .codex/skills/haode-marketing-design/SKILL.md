# HAODE Marketing Design

Before any image, price-list, product-family, website-product-media, or ad-image work, read `docs/HAODE_IMAGE_DESIGN_MASTER.md` first. Its `OWNER LOCKED` rules have priority over this workflow.

- Treat verified real products and packaging as locked layers; never redraw, regenerate, alter, imitate, or replace them with AI.
- Use generative or design tools only for the editable design layer.
- If a required real asset is unavailable, record `STATUS = ASSET MISSING`, complete every independent part, and do not generate a substitute.
- When the brief is clear, continue through final local output and mandatory QC without stopping after intermediate steps.

Use this skill for marketing visuals and ad copy.

## Rules

- Default language is Spanish.
- Default branding uses the official HAODE logo.
- Keep the tone commercial and clear for the Mexico mobile-repair wholesale market.
- Suggested sizes:
  - Facebook: `1080x1080`
  - Story/Reels/TikTok: `1080x1920`
- Highlight value terms such as `Calidad Profesional`, `Mayoreo`, `Envíos`, `CDMX`, and `Garantía`.
- Do not invent specs or claims that are not confirmed.
- For comparison graphics, emphasize HAODE quality advantages without false claims.
