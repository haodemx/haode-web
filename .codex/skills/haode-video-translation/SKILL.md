# HAODE Video Translation

Use this skill for video translation and social-video localization.

## Rules

- Translate Chinese content into Spanish by default.
- Generate subtitle files when needed.
- Keep product names, brands, models, and prices unchanged unless the user says otherwise.
- Preserve the product facts shown in the source video.
- Provide short TikTok/Reels-friendly wording when useful.
- Do not invent product claims or mix unrelated visuals.
